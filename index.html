⚙️ First-time setup — connect your Google Sheet
  1. Open your Health-Dashboard-Danny Google Sheet
  2. Click Share (top-right button) → under General access, choose Anyone with the link → set to Viewer → click Done
  3. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar (the normal sheet URL is fine)
  4. Paste it in the box below and click Save & Load

⚠️ "Anyone with the link" means readable by anyone who has the URL — not editable, and not searchable on Google. Your data stays yours.
Fallback: if loading fails, try File → Share → Publish to web → Weekly Log → CSV and paste that URL instead.
